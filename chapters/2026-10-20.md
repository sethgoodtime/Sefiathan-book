# **Day 292 — October 20, 2026**

Sefiathan woke on his twentieth morning of October and the reading landed like permission to stop dulling himself for others: The brain is an erogenous zone. Stop wasting time on people who can't keep up with your thoughts. The person who excites your mind will excite everything else. Look for sharp wit, not just sharp cheekbones.

He lay in bed feeling those words settle with recognition that made something spark in his chest. The past five days since naming the lie about going home had been strange—not difficult exactly, but disorienting. Like he'd been navigating by one star his entire journey and suddenly realized he'd been reading the wrong constellation. His family wouldn't change. The journey wasn't about becoming someone they could finally see. Maybe Millhaven wasn't even where he belonged. And once he'd stopped lying to himself about all of that, he'd had to sit with the question of what the journey was actually for if not triumphant homecoming.

But the reading wasn't asking him to figure that out yet. Was pointing at something more immediate. Stop wasting time on people who can't keep up with your thoughts. He'd been doing that his whole life—dulling himself, slowing down his mind, pretending his tangential observations and odd connections were annoying rather than interesting. Making himself smaller so other people would be comfortable with him. And not just with his family. With everyone. He'd learned early that the way his brain worked—fast and strange and making leaps that seemed obvious to him but baffling to others—was something to apologize for rather than celebrate.

Morning light filtered through his window and Sefiathan sat up slowly, feeling something shift in how he was holding his own mind. The person who excites your mind will excite everything else. What did that mean? He thought about the people he'd met on this journey. Torval and Mikken and Lyrei who'd taught him about multiple truths but hadn't really engaged with the speed of his thinking. The silent woman who'd made soup—beautiful connection but not intellectual. Maren at Crossroads Post who'd seen something in him but their conversation had been brief. Even Korrin, who he loved, who understood him deeply—Korrin's mind worked differently than his. More methodical. More grounded. Beautiful in its own way but not—not sharp in the way the reading was describing.

Downstairs Erenne was making breakfast and when Sefiathan entered the kitchen she was already smiling.

"You look like someone just gave you permission to be clever again."

"The reading said the brain is an erogenous zone. That I should stop wasting time on people who can't keep up with my thoughts. That the person who excites my mind will excite everything else." He poured himself tea. "That I should look for sharp wit, not just sharp cheekbones."

Erenne laughed, actually laughed, and Sefiathan felt something warm in his chest at the sound. "You've been making yourself smaller for a long time, haven't you? Pretending your brain doesn't work as fast as it does."

"My whole life. Because when I was a kid and I'd make connections between things, my father would tell me to focus. When I'd see patterns other people missed, Elara would roll her eyes. When I'd get excited about ideas, people would look at me like I was showing off or trying too hard or being deliberately difficult." He stopped, feeling old hurt surface. "So I learned to slow down. To not share all the thoughts. To pretend I was confused about things that seemed obvious to me. To make myself duller so other people would be comfortable."

"But your brain is an erogenous zone. The reading's not being metaphorical about that—intellectual excitement is its own kind of attraction. And you've been starving yourself of it by surrounding yourself with people who can't keep up."

They ate together and Sefiathan felt the Do list working through him. Post-it notes, Thought maps, Visioning. Leave evidence of your actual thinking process instead of hiding it. Thought maps—let yourself see the connections you're making even if they seem strange. Visioning—imagine what it would feel like to be with someone who excites your mind instead of someone you have to dull yourself for. All of it pointing toward the same thing. Stop wasting time on people who can't keep up with your thoughts.

"I don't think I've ever been with someone who excites my mind," he said quietly. "I've been attracted to people. Loved people. But I don't think I've ever had someone match my speed intellectually and felt that be—exciting. Erotic even. I thought that was just—not how I worked."

"Or maybe you've just never found someone sharp enough to make your brain light up. Because you've been hiding how sharp you are."

Morning stretched into midday and Sefiathan walked out into the grasslands feeling this permission settling through him like unfamiliar permission to be himself. Look for sharp wit, not just sharp cheekbones. He found his spot by the creek and sat down, and immediately his mind started racing the way it always did when he let it—making connections between the way water moved and the way wind-magic worked and the way Orvenna had taught him about deep currents and whether there were patterns underneath patterns underneath patterns that governed everything. His brain jumping from thought to thought with speed that he'd learned to hide because it made people uncomfortable.

But what if he didn't hide it? What if he found someone who could match that speed? Who found his tangential observations delightful instead of annoying? Who got excited when his brain made weird leaps because their brain worked similarly? The person who excites your mind will excite everything else. He'd never experienced that. Had never even considered it might be possible. Had assumed intellectual connection and romantic attraction were separate things and you just hoped to find someone you had enough of both with even if they never quite met.

The Don't list felt pointed. Browsing, Assumptions, Speakerphone. Don't just passively browse through people hoping to find someone adequate—actively seek sharp minds. Don't assume you're too much or too fast or too strange to find intellectual match. Don't broadcast yourself on speakerphone trying to reach everyone—find the people who can actually hear your frequency. The reading was giving permission to be selectively brilliant instead of universally dulled.

Afternoon brought Korrin to the creek and when he sat down Sefiathan turned to him with unusual directness.

"Can I ask you something honestly?"

"Always."

"Do you think I'm too much? Like—do you think my brain works too fast sometimes? That I make connections that don't make sense or jump between thoughts in ways that are hard to follow?"

Korrin was quiet for a moment. "Sometimes your brain works faster than I can track, yes. You'll make leaps that take me a minute to understand. But that's not you being too much—that's just how your mind works. It's actually one of the things I admire about you even when I can't quite follow the path you took to get there."

"But you can't always keep up."

"No. But that doesn't mean you should slow down for me. Just means we think differently." Korrin looked at him carefully. "What did the reading say?"

"That the brain is an erogenous zone. That I should stop wasting time on people who can't keep up with my thoughts. That the person who excites my mind will excite everything else."

"And you're realizing you've been dulling yourself to make other people comfortable."

"Yes. My whole life. Making myself slower and smaller and more confused than I actually am because people got uncomfortable when I was sharp."

"Then stop. Be as sharp as you are. Find someone who finds that exciting instead of threatening."

They sat together and Sefiathan felt something fundamental shifting in how he held his own intelligence. He had a fast mind. Made connections other people missed. Saw patterns that seemed obvious to him but baffling to others. And he'd been treating all of that like character flaw instead of gift. Like something to apologize for instead of celebrate. Like he needed to find people who would tolerate his sharpness instead of people who would be excited by it.

The person who excites your mind will excite everything else. He'd never let himself want that before. Had never even considered intellectual excitement could be erotic. Had thought attraction was about physical appeal and emotional connection and you just accepted that your mind would be lonely even in relationship. But the reading was saying otherwise. Was saying there were people out there who would match his speed. Whose sharp wit would excite him as much as sharp cheekbones. Who would make his brain light up and that lighting up would extend to everything else.

Evening brought them back to the waystation where Erenne had dinner ready. And when she asked about his day, Sefiathan answered with less apologetic qualification than usual.

"I've been realizing I've spent my whole life making myself duller so other people would be comfortable with me. Slowing down my thoughts. Hiding the connections I see. Pretending I'm more confused than I am. But the reading said to look for sharp wit, not just sharp cheekbones. That the person who excites my mind will excite everything else."

"And you've never let yourself want that before."

"No. Never even thought it was possible. Thought intellectual connection and romantic attraction were separate. That you just hoped to find enough of both even if neither quite met."

"But what if you found someone who matched your speed? Who made your brain light up?"

"Then I think—I think everything would light up."

Later in his room Sefiathan lay in bed feeling October moving forward. Twenty days into this month and learning about truths he'd been avoiding and now about sharpness he'd been hiding. About recognizing he had a fast, strange, brilliant mind that made connections other people missed. About understanding he'd been dulling himself his whole life to make others comfortable. About permission to stop wasting time on people who couldn't keep up with his thoughts. About the possibility that somewhere out there were people whose sharp wit would excite him as much as beauty. Who would make his brain light up and that would extend to everything else.

Stop wasting time on people who can't keep up with your thoughts. He would. Starting now. No more apologizing for being fast. No more hiding connections. No more pretending to be confused about things that seemed obvious. The person who excites your mind will excite everything else. He didn't know who that person was yet. Didn't know if he'd find them on this journey or after it. But he knew now what he was looking for. Sharp wit. Quick mind. Someone who matched his speed instead of asking him to slow down. Someone whose brain was as erogenous as his own. Someone who would light everything up.
